Federate by Cyberdione Labs

Privacy Policy

Effective October 2, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Cyberdione Labs Corporation (“Cyberdione,” “we,” or “us”) processes information when you use Federate to sign in with an AT Protocol account or use an account we host at pds.federate.to. Contact us at support@cyberdione.ai with privacy questions or requests. Connected applications make their own authorization decisions and have separate privacy practices.

1. Information we process

  • The handle or DID you enter and the DID and handle confirmed during AT Protocol authorization.
  • OAuth and OpenID Connect request state, authorization codes, tokens, browser-binding cookies, and related security information needed to complete sign-in.
  • Technical and operational records such as request time, IP address, user agent, errors, and security events, to operate and protect Federate.
  • Messages and contact details you send to support.
  • If we host your PDS account: your email address, account credentials and security settings, DID, handle, repository records, uploaded media, connected-app authorizations, and account-management requests.

If another provider hosts your AT Protocol account, your password is entered with that provider's PDS or authorization service, not with Federate's OpenID Connect sign-in. If we host your account, our PDS processes the password you submit to it for account access; Federate's OpenID Connect sign-in does not ask for that password.

2. How we use information

We use this information to verify your AT Protocol identity, issue identity claims to an application after the sign-in flow, prevent abuse, troubleshoot failures, maintain security, and answer support requests. For accounts hosted by us, we also use it to operate your PDS account, publish and synchronize the records you create, send account messages, and support account recovery. We do not make a connected application's authorization decision.

Where applicable privacy law requires a legal basis, we rely on providing the sign-in service you request, our legitimate interests in operating and securing it, and compliance with legal obligations. We seek consent where the law requires it.

3. Sharing and independent services

Federate communicates with your PDS or authorization service to complete sign-in. A registered application receives the identity claims it requested through the OpenID Connect flow, including your DID and, when available, your handle. If we host your account, its DID, handle, repository records, and media you publish can be accessed or copied by AT Protocol services and other network participants. Other services may keep copies even after you remove content from our PDS. Your email address and account password are not part of your public repository.

Infrastructure, backup, logging, and email-delivery providers may process information on our behalf to operate these services. We may disclose information when required by law or to protect the service and others. We do not sell personal information or use it for cross-context behavioral advertising.

When another provider hosts your account, its privacy notice governs its own processing. Connected applications and other AT Protocol services are independently operated and have their own privacy practices.

4. Cookies and retention

Federate uses essential first-party browser cookies to bind a sign-in request to your browser and protect the flow. Our PDS also uses authentication and security cookies when you use its account pages. These are not used for advertising. A sign-in transaction expires after a short period; expired records may remain until routine cleanup or backup expiration. Hosted account data remains while the account is active. After account or record deletion, copies in backups may remain until backup expiration, and public copies held by other services are outside our control. Operational and security records and support messages are retained as reasonably needed for service operation, abuse prevention, troubleshooting, and legal obligations.

5. Security and your choices

We use technical and organizational safeguards intended to protect information processed by our services. No service can guarantee perfect security. Depending on where you live, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, objection, or restriction of processing, or to complain to a privacy regulator. Contact support@cyberdione.ai; we may need to verify control of the relevant AT Protocol identity before acting. You can manage an account hosted by us through its PDS account pages, use the PDS account-deletion flow, or contact us for help. For accounts hosted elsewhere, contact that PDS or authorization service; contact connected applications about their own records.

6. Children

Federate is for people who are at least 13 and meet the minimum age required where they live. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has used Federate, contact us so we can investigate.

7. Changes and contact

We may update this Policy. We will post the revised version here with a new effective date and, when appropriate, provide additional notice. Questions and privacy requests may be sent to support@cyberdione.ai.
