Federate by Cyberdione Labs

Privacy Policy

Effective September 30, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Cyberdione Labs Corporation (“Cyberdione,” “we,” or “us”) processes information when you use Federate to sign in with an AT Protocol account. Contact us at support@cyberdione.ai with privacy questions or requests. Federate is an identity provider; connected applications make their own authorization decisions and have separate privacy practices.

1. Information we process

  • The handle or DID you enter and the DID and handle confirmed during AT Protocol authorization.
  • OAuth and OpenID Connect request state, authorization codes, tokens, browser-binding cookies, and related security information needed to complete sign-in.
  • Technical and operational records such as request time, IP address, user agent, errors, and security events, to operate and protect Federate.
  • Messages and contact details you send to support.

Your AT Protocol password is entered with your PDS or authorization service, not Federate. Federate does not ask you to enter it.

2. How we use information

We use this information to verify your AT Protocol identity, issue identity claims to an application after the sign-in flow, prevent abuse, troubleshoot failures, maintain security, and answer support requests. We do not make the application's authorization decision.

Where applicable privacy law requires a legal basis, we rely on providing the sign-in service you request, our legitimate interests in operating and securing it, and compliance with legal obligations. We seek consent where the law requires it.

3. Sharing and independent services

Federate communicates with your PDS or authorization service to complete sign-in. A registered application receives the identity claims it requested through the OpenID Connect flow, including your DID and, when available, your handle. Infrastructure and logging providers may process information on our behalf to operate Federate. We may disclose information when required by law or to protect the service and others. We do not sell personal information or use it for cross-context behavioral advertising.

Your PDS, authorization service, and connected applications are independently operated. Their privacy notices govern their own processing.

4. Cookies and retention

Federate uses essential first-party browser cookies to bind a sign-in request to your browser and protect the flow. These are used for authentication and security, not advertising. A sign-in transaction expires after a short period; expired records may remain until routine cleanup or backup expiration. Operational and security records, support messages, and backups are retained only as long as reasonably needed for their purposes, including service operation, abuse prevention, troubleshooting, and legal obligations.

5. Security and your choices

We use technical and organizational safeguards intended to protect information processed by Federate. No service can guarantee perfect security. Depending on where you live, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, objection, or restriction of processing, or to complain to a privacy regulator. Contact support@cyberdione.ai; we may need to verify control of the relevant AT Protocol identity before acting. You can also manage your AT Protocol account through your PDS or authorization service and contact connected applications about their own records.

6. Children

Federate is for people who are at least 13 and meet the minimum age required where they live. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has used Federate, contact us so we can investigate.

7. Changes and contact

We may update this Policy. We will post the revised version here with a new effective date and, when appropriate, provide additional notice. Questions and privacy requests may be sent to support@cyberdione.ai.
